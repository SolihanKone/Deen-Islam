Description:
Deen Islam helps you stay connected to your deen throughout the day.

Quran
• Madinah-style mushaf pages and a learn-as-you-read surah view
• Translations in English, Urdu, and French
• Recitation audio and optional translation voice
• Bookmarks and last-read page

Prayer and qibla
• Prayer times from your location
• Optional local salah reminders (no account required)
• Qibla compass

Worship
• Daily ayah, dua, and tasbeeh
• 99 Names of Allah
• Dark mode and Arabic, English, Urdu, or French app language

Location is used only to calculate prayer times and qibla, and stays on your device. There are no ads and no account to create.
